Focus Mode — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Focus Mode ("the app") is a productivity app that helps you block distracting apps during timed focus sessions.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All session history, allowlist settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device only and never leave it.

Advertising

The free version of Focus Mode displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Pro subscribers see no ads.

In-App Purchases

The app offers consumable purchases (early session unlock) and a Pro subscription, processed entirely through Google Play. We do not receive or store any payment information.

Accessibility Service

Focus Mode uses Android's Accessibility Service solely to detect which app is currently in the foreground, in order to redirect you when you open a blocked app during a session. The app does NOT read screen content, record keystrokes, access passwords or messages, or store or transmit any accessibility data.

VPN Service

Pro users may enable internet blocking via a local VPN. This VPN operates entirely on your device. No network traffic is inspected, stored, forwarded, or transmitted anywhere. The VPN only silently drops packets for non-exempt apps.

Permissions

  • Accessibility Service — foreground app detection only
  • Notifications — session timer updates
  • VPN (Pro) — local internet blocking, no data leaves the device
  • Exact Alarm — session end timer

Children's Privacy

Focus Mode is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated date.
